## Recommendation

The recorded 4b gate (first Haiku run) scored F1 1.00 on holdout+stress and F1 1.00 on stress.  `jev-1.14.0`: stress Jev alone F1 1.00 (TP 8/8, FP 0/4), regex OR Jev F1 1.00 (TP 8/8, FP 0/4); holdout+stress Jev alone F1 0.98, regex OR Jev F1 1.00. `jev-1.13.0`: stress Jev alone F1 1.00 (TP 8/8, FP 0/4), regex OR Jev F1 1.00 (TP 8/8, FP 0/4); holdout+stress Jev alone F1 0.91, regex OR Jev F1 1.00.

A Jev variant tied 4b on these drafts. Jev is cheaper and faster per call than a Haiku call, so it is a reasonable cheaper drop-in for the Haiku check, but a tie on 46 synthetic drafts is not evidence that it is better. Keep 4b as the default until Jev is rerun on real drafts. Every split is small and synthetic. Before switching in production, rerun on real drafts.
